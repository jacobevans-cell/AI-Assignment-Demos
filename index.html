What is this?

These are not screenshots or mockups. They are working browser-based activities created with AI and published as simple web pages. The goal is to demonstrate how teachers can take existing curriculum, standards, worksheets, readings, videos, or practice activities and redesign them into interactive learning experiences.

GAMIFIED MATH

Decimal Deception

Activity Description

Students navigate a space-station adventure while solving decimal problems. Correct answers help them complete missions, collect Power Crystals, avoid enemies, and unlock new areas.

Arizona Math Standard

6.NS.B.3 — Fluently add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit decimals using the standard algorithm.

Intentional Design

What if the same practice problems from a worksheet became part of a game? The academic skill remains the focus, but repetitive practice is built into missions, movement, challenges, immediate feedback, and progression.

INTERACTIVE LESSON

Volleyball Academy

Activity Description

Students work through a series of volleyball missions combining reading, videos, game situations, multiple-choice comprehension checks, drag-and-drop activities, immediate feedback, scoring, and progress tracking.

Learning Content

The lesson was created from existing instructional resources, including web articles, instructional reading, and YouTube videos. Students process the information and apply what they learn to interactive challenges.

Intentional Design

Instead of simply giving students links to articles and videos, the resources were transformed into one structured interactive assignment. Students read, watch, respond, sort, make decisions, and demonstrate understanding before progressing. Volleyball is simply the example.

GUIDED MATH

Long Division Quest

Activity Description

Students solve long-division problems one aligned step at a time. The program guides students through divide, multiply, subtract, and bring down while tracking accuracy, XP, streaks, problems completed, and hints used.

Instructional Support

Rather than simply marking an entire problem wrong, the activity focuses students on the exact digit and step they are working on. Visual grouping models help students understand how many groups fit into a number before entering the quotient digit.

Intentional Design

Long division is a multi-step process, so the interface was intentionally designed to mirror the algorithm. Students receive targeted prompts, optional hints, and visual grouping support exactly when they need it.

LITERACY + GAMEPLAY

Spelling Practice

Activity Description

One spelling list becomes a complete learning system. Students can study with instructional flash cards, practice through volleyball and soccer games, and complete an independent listen-and-spell test.

Word Study & Pedagogy

Each word was intentionally broken apart for instruction. Students study syllables, pronunciation, sound chunks, phonograms, spelling patterns, word structure, visual examples, and targeted spelling reminders before practicing independently.

Intentional Design

The flash cards make the structure of each word visible. Multi-letter phonograms are automatically highlighted, difficult patterns receive targeted “Watch For” hints, individual sound chunks can be heard aloud, and students can listen to the whole word and an example sentence.

Students can see it, hear it, break it apart, study the pattern, practice it, apply it in a game, and finally spell it independently.

How was this made?

The teacher does not need to start as a programmer. The process begins with curriculum, an instructional goal, and an idea for how students should experience the learning.

STEP 1
Start with instruction

Identify the standard, skill, lesson content, existing resource, worksheet, or practice students need to complete.

STEP 2
Design with AI

Describe the experience you want. AI can help build the webpage, interactions, feedback, visuals, scoring, games, and instructional supports.

STEP 3
Test, revise & publish

The teacher tests the assignment, requests changes, and continues refining it. The finished HTML can then be published with GitHub Pages and linked or embedded into Google Sites.
