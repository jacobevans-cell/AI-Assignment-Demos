GAMIFIED MATH

Decimal Deception

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Students navigate a space-station adventure while solving decimal problems. Correct answers help them complete missions, collect Power Crystals, avoid enemies, and unlock new areas.

ARIZONA MATH STANDARD

6.NS.B.3 — Fluently add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit decimals using the standard algorithm.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

What if the same practice problems from a worksheet became part of a game? The academic skill remains the focus, but repetitive practice is built into missions, movement, challenges, immediate feedback, and progression.

INTERACTIVE LESSON

Volleyball Academy

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Students work through volleyball missions combining reading, videos, game situations, multiple-choice comprehension checks, drag-and-drop activities, immediate feedback, scoring, and progress tracking.

LEARNING CONTENT

The lesson was created from existing instructional resources, including web articles, instructional reading, and YouTube videos. Students process the information and apply what they learn to interactive challenges.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

Instead of simply giving students links to articles and videos, the resources were transformed into one structured interactive assignment. Students read, watch, respond, sort, make decisions, and demonstrate understanding before progressing. Volleyball is simply the example.

GUIDED MATH PRACTICE

Long Division Quest

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Students solve long-division problems one aligned step at a time. The program guides them through Divide, Multiply, Subtract, and Bring Down while tracking accuracy, hints, streaks, progress, and XP.

INSTRUCTIONAL SUPPORT

Students work directly inside the long-division structure. Prompts focus attention on the exact digit and step they are working on, while visual grouping models help students understand how many groups fit into a number before entering the quotient digit.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

Long division is a multi-step process, so the interface was intentionally designed to mirror the algorithm. Students receive targeted prompts, optional hints, and visual grouping support exactly when they need it.

LITERACY & WORD STUDY

Spelling Practice Lab

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

One spelling list becomes a complete study system. Students can study detailed flash cards, practice through volleyball or soccer games, hear words and sentences aloud, and complete an independent spelling test.

WORD STUDY PEDAGOGY

Each word was intentionally broken apart to teach more than memorization. Students study syllables, pronunciation, sound chunks, phonograms, spelling patterns, word structure, visual examples, and targeted spelling reminders before practicing independently.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

The flash cards make the structure of each word visible. Multi-letter phonograms are automatically highlighted, difficult patterns receive targeted “Watch For” hints, and individual sound chunks can be heard aloud.

Students can see it, hear it, break it apart, study the pattern, practice it, apply it in a game, and finally spell it independently.

What could this look like at Explore?

The point isn't to replace curriculum or instruction with AI. The opportunity is to use AI to help teachers redesign materials they already have into experiences that give students more opportunities to interact, practice, receive feedback, and demonstrate understanding.

📄 Worksheet → Experience

Turn repetitive skill practice into missions, challenges, simulations, games, or guided practice.

📚 Content → Interaction

Combine readings, videos, and instructional resources into one structured learning experience.

🧠 Skill → Scaffolding

Build hints, models, immediate feedback, and step-by-step support directly into independent student practice.

🎯 Idea → Prototype

A teacher can describe an instructional idea, test it, revise it, and turn it into a working classroom tool without starting as a programmer.

How were these built?

The process begins with instruction, not code.

STEP 1
Start with the learning

Identify the standard, skill, lesson content, student need, or existing classroom material.

STEP 2
Design the experience

Describe how students should interact with the content, where they need support, and what successful learning should look like.

STEP 3
Build, test & revise

AI helps generate the webpage. The teacher tests it, identifies problems, requests changes, and continues revising until the activity works for students.
