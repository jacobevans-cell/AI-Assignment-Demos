Explore examples of interactive classroom assignments created with AI. Open a demo, test it like a student, and imagine what you could build for your own class.

Built as a proof of concept for teachers and staff.

What is this?

These are not screenshots or mockups. They are working browser-based activities created with AI and published as simple web pages. The goal is to show how teachers can turn an idea, lesson, review, or practice activity into something students can actually interact with.

How this concept works

The technical setup is much simpler than the finished activities make it look.

Step 1
Describe the assignment

Tell AI the skill, grade level, content, style, and type of interaction you want.

Step 2
Generate the webpage

AI creates the HTML, styling, questions, interactions, and game logic.

Step 3
Publish and share

Upload the HTML to GitHub Pages and embed or link it from Google Sites.
