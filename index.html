THE EXPLORE CONNECTION

Build the Flavor

Explore's model recognizes that students do not all need the exact same experience to reach the same destination. AI can help extend that philosophy by allowing teachers to preserve the standard and rigor while changing the context, scaffolding, modality, challenge, feedback, or student pathway.

1. Keep the Target

Start with the standard, skill, text, vocabulary, assessment target, movement objective, or learning goal.

→
2. Design the Flavor

Change the theme, context, interaction, scaffolding, modality, difficulty, or type of practice.

→
3. Reach the Same Mastery

The experience may look different, but the underlying learning expectation remains intentional and measurable.

Same target. Different path.

Students can work toward the same academic or performance goal through different themes, supports, levels of independence, and types of interaction. AI can make those different pathways far more practical for teachers to design, revise, and reuse.

How were these built?

The process begins with instructional design, not programming.

STEP 1
Start with the learning

Identify the standard, student need, resource, lesson, assessment, practice problem, or instructional challenge.

STEP 2
Design the experience

Decide what students should do, where support is needed, how feedback should work, and what flavor best supports the goal.

STEP 3
Build, test & revise

AI helps build the technology. The teacher tests, adjusts rigor, improves scaffolds, and determines whether the experience supports learning.
