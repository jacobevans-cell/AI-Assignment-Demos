What is this?

These are not screenshots or mockups. They are working browser-based activities created with AI and published as simple web pages. The goal is to show how teachers can turn an idea, lesson, review, or practice activity into something students can actually interact with.

GAMIFIED MATH

Decimal Deception

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Students navigate a space-station adventure while solving decimal problems. Correct answers help them complete missions, collect Power Crystals, avoid enemies, and unlock new areas.

ARIZONA MATH STANDARD

6.NS.B.3 — Fluently add, subtract, multiply, and divide multi-digit decimals using the standard algorithm.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

What if the same practice problems from a worksheet became part of a game? The academic skill remains the focus, but repetitive practice is built into missions, movement, challenges, immediate feedback, and progression.

INTERACTIVE LESSON

Volleyball Academy

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Students work through a series of volleyball missions that combine reading, videos, game situations, multiple-choice comprehension checks, drag-and-drop activities, immediate feedback, scoring, and progress tracking.

LEARNING CONTENT

The lesson was created from existing instructional resources, including web articles, instructional reading, and YouTube videos. Students process the information and apply what they learn to interactive challenges.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

Instead of simply giving students links to articles and videos, the resources were transformed into one structured interactive assignment. Students read, watch, respond, sort, make decisions, and demonstrate understanding before progressing. Volleyball is simply the example.

GUIDED MATH PRACTICE

Long Division Quest

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

Students solve long division problems one digit at a time using the Divide → Multiply → Subtract → Bring Down sequence. Each digit is entered directly into its correct place-value position.

LEARNING OBJECTIVE

Build accuracy and fluency with multi-digit long division while reinforcing place value, multiplication facts, subtraction, procedural order, and mathematical reasoning.

INTENTIONAL DESIGN

Instead of marking an entire quotient wrong, the activity responds to each individual step. Students receive immediate feedback, targeted prompts, optional hints, and visual grouping support exactly when they need it.

MULTIMODAL LITERACY

Spelling Practice Lab

ACTIVITY DESCRIPTION

One spelling list becomes a complete learning system. Students study with interactive flash cards, practice through volleyball and soccer games, hear words and sentences read aloud, and finish with a traditional listen-and-type spelling test.

WORD STUDY & PEDAGOGY

Each word is intentionally broken down for instruction. Students examine syllables, pronunciation, sound-it-out chunks, phonograms, spelling patterns, definitions, example sentences, and visual examples before being expected to spell independently.

INTENTIONAL SCAFFOLDING

The flash cards make the structure of each word visible. Multi-letter phonograms are automatically highlighted, difficult patterns receive targeted “Watch For” hints, individual sound chunks can be heard aloud, and students can listen to both the complete word and an example sentence.

WHY THIS MATTERS

Students can see it, hear it, break it apart, study the pattern, practice it, apply it in a game, and finally spell it independently. The games are the engagement layer; the word-analysis instruction underneath them is the instructional foundation.

How was this made?

The teacher doesn't need to be a programmer. The process starts with the learning goal, the instructional content, and an idea for how students should experience it.

STEP 1
Describe the idea

Give AI the standard, skill, grade level, lesson content, existing resources, and the type of experience you want students to have.

STEP 2
Build & revise

AI generates the webpage. The teacher tests it, identifies problems, adds instructional supports, and requests changes until the assignment works the way they want.

STEP 3
Publish & share

The finished HTML file is published with GitHub Pages and can be linked or embedded directly into Google Sites for students or staff to access.
